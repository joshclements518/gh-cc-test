## 1. Add Explore Wellness Chip
Implement the Wellness filter chip in the Harborline Navigator app to filter venues categorized as Spa or Fitness from `MockCatalog.venues`, while retaining existing category chips.

### Acceptance criteria
- The Wellness filter chip is visible on the Explore screen.
- Users can activate the Wellness filter chip.
- Only venues categorized as Spa or Fitness are displayed when the filter is active.
- Existing category chips remain functional and unaffected.
